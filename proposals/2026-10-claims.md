# Medical aid claims: what changed, what still needs the database, and how automatic submission would work

Drafted 2026-10-06. Companion to `proposals/2026-10-claims.sql` (not applied) and
`supabase/functions-proposals/auto-submit-claims/index.ts` (not deployed).

## Why

The practice owner's rule: **patients must never get a wrong price**, and every billed medical aid
visit must reach the fund as a claim the fund will pay. The fund pays only when the claim has the
right tariff code and price, an ICD-10 diagnosis ("no ICD-10, no pay"), the right member and
practitioner details, and arrives before the fund's deadline.

## What works now (no database change needed)

**Prices** - the service selectors and invoice saving now use one calculation
(`src/utils/billing/pricing.ts`). The Add/Edit visit tab screens still have their own dental pricing
until they are switched to it (listed separately for their owner):

- Only tariffs that are active and in effect on the service date are offered. If the tariff list has
  the same code twice, the newest version wins; two versions starting the same day with different
  prices are withheld (and listed) instead of guessed.
- A line costs unit price x quantity, worked out in whole cents (no more N$ 406.20000000000005).
- Codes that cannot be multiplied are billed with quantity 1 (another tooth = another line).
- "Sometimes" and "progressive" codes are priced unit x quantity and flagged for review. The old
  screens invented 50% or 80% prices for extra units - no tariff says that.
- The medical aid share and the patient share always add up to the line total.
- There is no hard-coded price list any more. Before, if the tariff list failed to load, screens
  quietly billed from a 2025 list (and one screen from made-up codes such as "M001 N$ 450").
- Invoices are checked before they are saved: a line that is not from the tariff list, a quantity
  that breaks the code's rule, or a total that is not unit x quantity stops the invoice with a clear
  message, and no empty invoice is left behind.
- When an invoice is created, medical aid or private is decided by the patient's medical aid record,
  not by the screen (some screens marked every dental line as medical aid, so private patients were
  not charged).

**ICD-10** - each service line on the billing screen has an ICD-10 entry (primary + up to 9
secondary codes, built-in list of common GP and dental codes, free entry with format checks such as
`J06.9`). Until the database has columns for them, the codes stay on the screen; the claims screen
asks the doctor to confirm codes per claim line and saves the primary code on the batch line
(`batch_details.diagnostic_code`, which already exists).

**Claims** - Finance > Batches (`ClaimsBatches`):

- *Open batches*: one per fund. Each line is checked: membership number, main member surname and
  initials, patient date of birth, ID number and reference, gender, practice number, treating
  doctor's HPCNA number, 4-digit tariff code, ICD-10, amounts, and the fund deadline (NHP 4 months,
  NMC 120 days, PSEMAS 180 days, otherwise 120 days until configured). Errors block submission.
- The claim file is built in the exact layout the funds accepted from the practice's previous
  software (format 1.6, 53 fields per line, CRLF). The old generators in the repo had drifted from it
  (54 fields, amounts without decimals, wrong header field), which would have shifted the patient ID
  number into the wrong column.
- Preview (with a field-by-field inspector), download, and **Submit to fund**: emailed with the file
  attached through the centralized email gateway, after a confirmation. Every attempt is recorded in
  `batch_submissions`; the batch is marked sent with the exact file that was sent; a batch is never
  sent twice.
- *Remove an invoice from an unsent batch* (to correct its billing): deletes only that invoice's
  lines in that batch and clears the invoice's batch link, after a confirmation. Sent batches are
  locked. When the invoice's lines are saved again, the database normally re-batches it.
- *Not yet batched*: medical aid invoices from the last 12 months that are in no batch (they would
  never be claimed); staff add them to the fund's open batch. Each invoice is re-checked just before
  it is added, because `add_invoice_to_batch()` would otherwise add its lines a second time.
- *History*: all batches with status and submission attempts; the sent file can be downloaded again.

## What the SQL proposal adds

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| `invoice_services.icd10_primary`, `icd10_secondary` | Keep the ICD-10 codes the doctor enters when billing, instead of only on screen. |
| `batch_details.diagnostic_codes_secondary` | Carry secondary codes onto the claim line. |
| `visit_diagnoses` | The visit's diagnoses as data (one primary, up to 9 secondary, who confirmed them). |
| `medical_aid_fund_rules` | Deadline, claims email and channel per fund, maintained by an administrator instead of in code. |
| `claim_line_responses` | What the fund paid or rejected per line, with the rejection code, and a worklist of lines to resubmit. |
| `practice_claim_settings`, `batch_headers.auto_submit_error` | Switch for automatic submission per practice, and the reason a batch was held. |
| Indexes | Fast "not yet batched" and batch screens. |
| RLS | Staff see only their practice's rows; admins and doctors change diagnoses and settings; fund rules are shared and changed only by super admins. |

The script runs in a transaction that ends in `ROLLBACK`; nothing happens until it is reviewed,
tested on a branch database and switched to `COMMIT`. Section 8 lists corrections for the database
function `add_invoice_to_batch()` (it fills every claim line with the HPCNA number `DEN00417`, the
invoice date instead of the visit date, and the patient's database id instead of the 7-digit
reference). The claims screen already corrects these in the file; compare with the live function
before replacing it.

## Automatic submission (design)

`supabase/functions-proposals/auto-submit-claims/index.ts` is outside `supabase/functions` so it
cannot be deployed by accident. When enabled it would, on a schedule:

1. Take practices with `practice_claim_settings.auto_submit_enabled = true`.
2. Take their open batches that have been open at least `min_days_open` days, or whose oldest line
   is within 28 days of the fund deadline.
3. Lock each batch (`active` -> `sending` in one conditional update), so a second run or a person
   pressing Submit at the same time cannot send it twice.
4. Validate with the same code as the screen. Only ICD-10 codes saved on the lines count;
   suggestions from visit notes always wait for a doctor.
5. Valid: email the file through `centralized-email-gateway`, record `batch_submissions`, mark the
   batch `sent`. Invalid: put the batch back to `active`, store the reasons in `auto_submit_error`,
   and email the practice what to fix. Email failure: retry next run, stop after 3 attempts.

It starts in dry-run mode (`AUTO_SUBMIT_DRY_RUN` is "true" unless set to "false"): it only reports
what it would do.

### Setup steps (after the SQL is applied)

1. Deploy: copy the folder to `supabase/functions/auto-submit-claims` and deploy it. The function
   imports the shared billing code from `src/utils/billing` and
   `src/components/finance/claims/buildClaimLines.ts` by relative path; if your Supabase CLI does
   not bundle files outside `supabase/`, copy those files into `supabase/functions/_shared/billing/`
   and adjust the imports.
2. Secrets: `supabase secrets set AUTO_SUBMIT_CRON_SECRET=<long random value> AUTO_SUBMIT_DRY_RUN=true`.
3. Store the same secret in Vault: `select vault.create_secret('<value>', 'auto_submit_cron_secret');`
4. Schedule with pg_cron + pg_net (Fridays 16:00 UTC = 18:00 in Namibia):

```sql
select cron.schedule(
  'auto-submit-claims',
  '0 16 * * 5',
  $$
  select net.http_post(
    url := 'https://<project-ref>.supabase.co/functions/v1/auto-submit-claims',
    headers := jsonb_build_object(
      'Content-Type', 'application/json',
      'x-cron-secret', (select decrypted_secret from vault.decrypted_secrets where name = 'auto_submit_cron_secret')
    ),
    body := '{}'::jsonb
  );
  $$
);
```

   If the function keeps JWT verification on, also send `Authorization: Bearer <anon key>` from Vault.
5. Run in dry-run mode for two cycles, compare its report with the batches staff send by hand, then
   set `AUTO_SUBMIT_DRY_RUN=false` and switch it on per practice in `practice_claim_settings`.
6. Make sure `batch_headers.status` allows `sending` (see the query in the SQL file).

## Decisions needed

- **MediSwitch.** Funds in Namibia increasingly expect claims through MediSwitch Namibia, which
  requires accredited software. Until HPMS is accredited, claims go by email in the legacy file
  format. Accreditation would change the submission channel (`medical_aid_fund_rules.submission_channel`),
  the header software name/version (`batch_headers.source_software`, currently `ONEHEALTH 01.00.00`
  where the old files said `EMINANCE 05.06.00`), and real-time line responses.
- **Fund claims addresses.** `medical_aid_providers.email` must be each fund's claims intake address
  (not a general enquiries address). Confirm them with each fund; submission is blocked when missing.
- **Deadlines and fund references.** Confirm NHP 4 months, NMC 120 days, PSEMAS 180 days, and the
  `medical_aid_reference` codes for PSEMAS and the other funds (MAG1, MAG2, NAMM, PROS).
- **Secondary ICD-10 codes in the file.** The accepted layout has one diagnosis field per line; the
  file carries the primary code. Ask the funds / MediSwitch how secondary codes are sent.
- **Dental rules.** 8346 (restorative material) is billed at 10% of its procedure; a per-tooth price
  table elsewhere gave different prices and was removed. The 6 "progressive" and the "sometimes"
  codes need their real rules from the NAMAF schedule; until then they are priced unit x quantity and
  flagged.
- **Trailer total.** The old software wrote totals over N$ 999.99 with a thousands comma
  (`23,652.41`), which splits the field; HPMS writes `23652.41`. Confirm with a fund or MediSwitch.
- **Claim files contain personal data.** The email gateway logs attachments in
  `practice_email_logs`, and the sent file is kept in `batch_headers.generated_file_content` for
  audit. Agree how long these are kept.
